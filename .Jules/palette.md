## 2026-08-06 - Dark Theme & Scroll-Animated Accessibility
**Learning:** In highly customized or dark-themed portfolios that rely on transition animations (like opacity/transform fades via IntersectionObserver), standard keyboard navigation can trigger severe "invisible focus traps" if focus moves into elements that are currently faded out. Additionally, dark backgrounds require explicit, high-contrast `:focus-visible` outline indicators to prevent keyboard users from losing track of their focus position.
**Action:** Always intercept focusin events on scroll-animated containers to instantly force their visibility (opacity: 1, transform: translateY(0)), and define strong `:focus-visible` styling (`outline: 2px solid var(--accent); outline-offset: 4px;`) across interactive elements.
