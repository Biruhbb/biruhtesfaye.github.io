## 2026-10-15 - Contact Copy-to-Clipboard Buttons
**Learning:** Adding dedicated copy-to-clipboard buttons next to contact details with clear temporary visual feedback ("Copied!") provides a frictionless experience for users copying emails and phone numbers while preserving standard mailto/tel link behavior.
**Action:** Always wrap contact links and copy actions in structured card containers, hide decorative contact icons with `aria-hidden="true"`, and grant explicit ARIA labels to copy buttons for screen reader clarity.
